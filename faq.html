Frequently asked questions

Buying, downloading and playing

How fast are new songs available?

New Jewish music is available as sheet music within 24–72 hours of its release.

How do I get my sheet music?

Right after checkout you get a link to download the PDF, and a copy is sent to your email.

How can I pay?

Checkout runs securely through Payhip, which accepts major credit cards, PayPal and other standard payment options. Your card details never reach us.

Can I download my music again later?

Yes. Sign in to Payhip with the email you used at checkout to see your orders and download them again.

Can I print it or use it on a tablet?

Yes. Every sheet is a standard PDF, so you can print it or open it on any phone, tablet or computer.

Can I buy several songs at once?

Yes. Tap the cart button on each song you want, then check out once from the cart.

What do the key, BPM and time signature tell me?

The song’s key, its tempo in beats per minute, and how the beats are grouped, so you can check a sheet suits you before you buy. Each song page also shows a preview of the sheet.
